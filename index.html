Fortnite only kept getting more and more players over the next few years. Now having pro players in their game.

The Fortnite world cup

The Fortnite world cup was one of the biggest gaming events in history. Having a total prize-pool of 30 million US dollars. The winner Bugha (Kyle Giersdorf) won a total of 3 million US dollars.

After the World Cup

Chapter 2 had just begun in 2019. Thats when the game began to really take off having 7-8 million concurring players.

Chapter 3

Fortnite Chapter 3 introduced a brand‑new flipped island, new mechanics, and four seasons released between December 2021 and December 2022

Chapter 4

Fortnite Chapter 4 is the fourth major chapter of Fortnite: Battle Royale, featuring a new map called Asteria, unique gameplay mechanics, and a series of themed seasons from December 2022 onward.

Chapter 5

Fortnite Chapter 5 introduces a new island, major gameplay changes, and multiple seasons beginning with Season 1 (Dec 3 2023 Mar 8 2024) featuring “The Underground vs. The Society,” followed by Season 3 (May 24 2024 Aug 15 2024) themed around an apocalyptic wasteland.

The present

The latest version of Fortnite is in Chapter 7, Season 4: Override, and the game is currently celebrating Halloween with its Fortnitemares event. The season is nearing its end, so there's a lot happening in-game.
